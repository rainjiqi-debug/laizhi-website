About Laizhi Trading

上海来之贸易有限公司总部位于上海，是一家专注于保险残值回收的专业贸易公司。过去十年中，我们在保险残值回收领域深耕细作，服务网络覆盖中国全境及亚太地区，处理过涵盖消费品、食品原材料、化工品、机械设备、大宗散货、新能源设备及跨境电商货物等全部主要品类。

Shanghai Laizhi Trading Co., Ltd. is a specialized trading company headquartered in Shanghai, China, focused on insurance salvage recovery. Over the past decade, we have built deep expertise in salvage recovery across China and the Asia-Pacific region, handling all major cargo categories including consumer goods, food and raw materials, chemicals, machinery and equipment, bulk commodities, new energy equipment, and cross-border e-commerce goods.

我们提供从现场勘查、残值评估、买家匹配到资金回收的全流程服务，并具备保税区操作能力——这一独特优势使我们能够为保险公司和公估机构提供残值出口处置的完整解决方案。

We provide end-to-end service from on-site survey, salvage valuation, buyer matching to fund recovery, and possess bonded zone operational capability — a unique advantage that enables us to offer complete salvage export disposal solutions for insurance companies and survey firms.

津巴布韦45MW光伏项目残值回收

Zimbabwe 45MW PV Project Salvage Recovery

🇵🇦 津巴布韦 / Zimbabwe 新能源设备 New Energy

津巴布韦45MW光伏项目中，28,080片光伏组件因运输及质保问题形成残值货物。我方直接对接非洲及中东地区光伏运维公司和二手组件买家，完成跨国买家匹配，实现残值回款闭环。

In a 45MW solar project in Zimbabwe, 28,080 PV modules became salvage goods due to transport damage and warranty issues. We directly engaged PV O&M companies and secondary module buyers across Africa and the Middle East, completing cross-border buyer matching and full salvage recovery.

核心价值：跳过国内处置环节，直接将残值货物匹配至海外终端买家，最大化残值回收价值。 Key Value: Bypassed domestic disposal and directly matched salvage goods to overseas end-buyers, maximizing recovery value.
